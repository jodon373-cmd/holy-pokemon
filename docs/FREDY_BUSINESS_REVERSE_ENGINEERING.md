## What the operation actually does

**1. Sourcing from grade-specific upside:** scans/vintage and selected modern sellers. Targets graded acquisition labels whose exact variant may have a higher sale outcome in another holder. Finds high-conviction PSA8/9, legacy CGC and BGS examples, sometimes multiple copies of identical cards but different certs. Potential sell price is not the same as current verified sales.

**2. Microscopic pre-purchase image triage:** reviews front/back PSA scans, magnifies whitening, border dots, edge, surface streaks, holo scratching, centering and slab artifacts, rejects some otherwise appealing expensive cards. April rejected shadowless Venusaur PSA8, FRLG Venusaur ex PSA8 and 1st Edition Chansey PSA7; May rejected Ho-Oh 1st, Espeon ex, Base Venusaur; July rejected Charizard PSA8 with rear red streak and Dark Charizard PSA9 with surface concerns. These are **rejections, not observed grader failures**. Our AI model should use them as expert-inspection preference/risk features only.

**3. Routes to the best priced grading slab:** PSA8→CGC10/Pristine, PSA9→PSA10, BGS9.5→PSA, CGC→CGC second attempts, sometimes CGC→PSA/BGS; also raw-to-graded wins and direct no-regrade flips. Label-era and subgrade distinctions matter; do not mix conversion datasets.

**4. Retrying selected failures:** Rayquaza ex EX Dragon PSA9→CGC9→CGC10 sale $3,549 with reported $2,220.65 gain in June; September Umbreon EX Delta Species PSA9→CGC9→CGC Pristine10 $11,700 sale. But September 1st Ed Articuno PSA9→CGC9→CGC9 sale $330 and reported -$447.51 loss; Japanese Sabrina's Gengar CGC8.5→CGC8. Every attempt costs money and each regrade is the SAME physical copy.

**5. Cash and inventory scaling:** May reported ~$158k cards in grading/prep; August $152,276.43 in grading/prep; September $316,388.86 estimated ready-to-sell plus $171,465.15 submission costs. September ~$487,854 combined inventory value and grading-related costs is not bank capital or realized profit. A small $2k pilot **must not imitate** this capital-heavy throughput; use cash-at-risk, age and grading-queue limits.

**6. Sales-channel optimization and liquidation:** February 63/66 via eBay and 3 PayPal. March 54 eBay, 8 Fanatics, 2 PayPal. April 37 eBay, 6 Fanatics. July 22 eBay, 15 Fanatics, 1 PayPal, with auctions used to release capital. August 33 eBay, 10 Fanatics, 14 Arizona TCG, 1 PayPal. September 79 Fanatics and 41 Arizona TCG, 0 direct eBay. September 97 final CGC slabs grossed $156,367.50; final slab doesn't determine original regrading route. Fredy's videos do NOT prove he used PSA Vault; Holy Pokémon's PSA Vault path is an additional strategy.

## Canonical strategy engine

1. DISCOVER: exact exit-first reverse queries, including cheap and premium, WOTC Japanese 1996–2007, EX-era/stamps and high-demand modern; stage interesting candidates even without comps.
2. RESOLVE: exact card number, edition, holo, stamped finish, language, set, label version, cert and specific physical copy.
3. INSPECT: original front/back high-res; four BGS subgrades and defects; unknown remains unknown. Label-hidden, leakage-safe AI assessment with abstention.
4. PRICE: independent dated completed comps by grader and grade, full fee and liquidity differences, no video-derived present price.
5. ROUTE: direct as-is sale, administrative CGC reholder, PSA intact cross with failed paid fee, crack PSA8/9/10, CGC 8–Pristine10, BGS, PSA Vault hold/sell/withdraw/regrade, normal venue resale.
6. COMPARE: incremental net vs sale original now, not simply new holder paper value minus historical cost. Include taxes, grading surcharge, travel/shipping, insurance, channel fees, hold time, downgrade and altered cases.
7. ALLOCATE: $2,000 total pilot at-risk, remaining $5,000 untouched, active grading pipeline/cash cycle; no purchases automatically.
8. SELL: choose Fanatics, Arizona TCG, eBay, PSA Vault where access/costs permit; avoid double-charging PSA consignment fees. Only actual recorded payouts enter realized net profit.
9. LEARN: store original prediction, actual grade, condition surprise, exit platform, final cash receipt, sourcing success; retrain/evaluate on physically isolated holdouts, not video frame duplicates.
