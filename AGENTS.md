# Holy Pokémon — repository operating rules

## User-approved scope as of 2026-10-28
This is the NEW private repository for the eventual Holy Pokémon bot. **At present: prepare and protect the research foundation, not an unsolicited bot implementation or a master Codex prompt.** Wait for the user's final build instruction before implementing scanner/product code.

1. **Preserve every source file.** ZIPs, raw WebM recordings, transcript logs, metadata, screenshots and condition images are immutable inputs. Do not overwrite, retouch, modify, regrade by image manipulation or delete originals. Keep huge binary research assets out of Git; reference them via manifest, hashes and relative local paths. Don't commit auth tokens, API keys, login cookies or private credentials.
2. **Treat research evidence accurately.** Differentiate source-caption claims, values shown on-screen, after-fee realized cash sales, unsold estimated value, asking price, purchase cost, grading cost, and verified outside-market comps. Missing fields remain NULL, not $0 or guesses. Creator's stated profit may be an *estimated value spread*.
3. **Dedup hierarchically.** Keep separate IDs for (a) video/source/recapture, (b) physical card copy, (c) grading submission/event, (d) a reported or verified sale. Repeated CGI/PSA grades of the *same physical card* must stay in one history, not appear as independent card samples. Same card identity across two videos is not enough to prove same physical copy: link conditionally, track confidence and audit trace. 546-card grade study and two-year gem-rate trends are **aggregates**, not itemized extra examples. Some monthly cases overlap with public video outcomes. Repeated downloads named `(1)`/`(2)` may be byte duplicates: SHA-256 dedup rather than count twice.
4. **Do not treat selected creator submissions as population-wide grading odds.** Build data structures suitable for predictive modeling and evaluation; require measured out-of-sample validation before claiming calibrated probabilities. Can still use creator examples immediately as heuristic evidence and condition exemplars. Keep One Piece BGS 9.5→PSA evidence separate from Pokémon; never generalize its 85% result to Pokémon.
5. **Inspect cards honestly.** Source photos are never enhanced to conceal flaws. Examine front/back alignment, centering, edges, whitening, corner damage, scratches and foil/surface damage; include obscured surfaces and slab glare as uncertainty. Use BGS subgrades; do not equate legacy CGC 9.5 with BGS 9.5 or CGC Pristine 10 with ordinary Gem Mint 10. Vintage set/edition, language, finish, stamp, corrected-error variant and collector number are part of exact identity.
6. **Preserve research vs. approval lanes.** Research leads can rank on potential upside even with absent comps/cost quotes/poor images; label insufficient information clearly. Trades need validated seller/listing, dated exact-variant sold comps, condition evidence, graded route alternatives, known full costs, downside and liquidity; otherwise NOT trade-approved. Don't let an over-strict validation gate hide exploratory candidates.
7. **Market data and permission.** Prefer eBay approved/authorized APIs; only use access actually granted. Other authorized channels (PSA Vault, PSA certificates if documented, manual Seller Hub/Product Research exports, PriceCharting or user-provided exports) must be isolated by provider adapter with source/date/provenance. Do not bypass service terms, CAPTCHA, access controls, or fabricate API availability. Market-price history must come from actual dated transactions, not YouTube creator values alone.
8. **Budget and actions.** Research-only until the user specifically authorizes purchases. Shared first-test budget $2,000 all-in; $5,000 reserve untouched. No auto-purchases, bids, credentials exposure, paid API spend or risky external operations without explicit approval.
9. **Use the old scanner carefully.** `jodon373-cmd/pokemon-market-scanner` is a read-only reference during foundation work. Reuse only reviewed/tested ideas or modules with explicit provenance, proper licensing and updated unit tests. Never modify, delete, rewrite or migrate old-repo state unless specifically requested.
10. **Developer efficiency.** Start by reading README, docs and research/manifest.csv. Inventory available ZIPs, verify hashes and ZIP integrity, and report missing assets. Build deterministic ingestion and evidence provenance before marketplace acquisition logic; synthesize no fake historical results. Don't repeatedly reparse multi-GB video when a structured CSV and cited evidence frames already exist. Maintain tests, explicit uncertainty and clear implementation status.
